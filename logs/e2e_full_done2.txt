==== FULL DONE A: PGDUN 的 PGSA 模块怎么实现 ====
PGDUN achieves state-of-the-art performance in hyperspectral reflectance reconstruction under mixed illumination, demonstrated through superior PSNR (39.62/43.10 dB), SSIM (0.984/0.990), and SAM (4.88°/4.69°) on two real test scenes, outperforming all compared methods including SPECAT-PAN, DAUHST-PAN, RDLUF-PAN, RCUMP-PAN, SSR-PAN, In2SET, CIDNet, and ADRNN. Its efficiency is competitive with only 2.13M parameters and 83.36G FLOPs. The method addresses illumination-induced sensing matrix mismatch and illumination-reflectance coupling via a Prompt-Guided Spectral Attention (PGSA) module and contrastive-learning-derived illumination-aware prompts. Visual results confirm improved spatial detail recovery (e.g., character legibility on cups, flower edges) and higher spectral correlation with reference spectra. Robustness to real-world CASSI acquisition characteristics—including 11–12-bit shot noise—is validated through synthetic training injection and real-data evaluation.
